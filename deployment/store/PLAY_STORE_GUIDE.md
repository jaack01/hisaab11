## Asset Requirements

### App Icon

**Specifications:**
- Size: 512 x 512 pixels
- Format: PNG (32-bit)
- No transparency
- Rounded corners will be applied automatically

**Design Guidelines:**
- Clear, recognizable symbol
- Works well at small sizes
- Represents accounting/ledger concept
- Professional appearance
- Follows Material Design principles

**Suggested Icon Concept:**
- ₹ (Rupee symbol) with ledger/notebook
- Calculator with Indian touch
- Simple "H" monogram with financial elements

## ASO (App Store Optimization) Tips

1. **Keywords**: Research and use relevant keywords in title and description
2. **Screenshots**: Show app in action with clear, annotated screenshots
3. **Icon**: Create memorable, professional icon
4. **Reviews**: Encourage satisfied users to leave positive reviews
5. **Updates**: Regular updates show active development
6. **Localization**: Translate listing to Hindi for better reach
7. **Ratings**: Respond to all reviews professionally
