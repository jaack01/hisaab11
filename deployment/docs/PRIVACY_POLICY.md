## Data We Collect

### Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Hisaab stores all your business data locally on your device. This includes:

1. **Customer Information**
   - Names, phone numbers, email addresses
   - Physical addresses
   - Transaction history
   - Account balances

2. **Business Data**
   - Business name and details
   - Transaction records (credits and debits)
   - Invoice information
   - Items/Products catalog
   - Expense records
   - Reminder settings

3. **Application Settings**
   - Language preferences
   - Theme preferences
   - Date and currency formats
   - Backup settings

### Data Not Collected

- We do NOT collect any personal data on our servers
- We do NOT track your location
- We do NOT access your contacts (unless you explicitly grant permission)
- We do NOT share your data with third parties
- We do NOT use analytics or tracking tools

## How We Use Your Data

All data is used solely for the functionality of the app:

1. **Customer Management**: To help you track customers and their balances
2. **Transaction Recording**: To maintain accurate financial records
3. **Report Generation**: To create business insights and PDF reports
4. **Reminders**: To notify you of pending payments
5. **Backup**: To allow you to backup and restore your data

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage

- All data is stored locally on your device using SQLite database
- Data is stored in your device's application data directory
- Only the Hisaab app can access this data

### Security Measures

We implement security measures to protect your data:

1. **Data Encryption**: Database can be encrypted on device
2. **Access Control**: Data is sandboxed within the app
3. **Secure Deletion**: Deleted items are properly removed
4. **No Cloud Sync**: Your data never leaves your device (unless you create backup)

### Backups

- You can create manual backups that are stored on your device
- Backups are stored in your device's documents directory
- You are responsible for backing up to external storage if desired
- We recommend keeping backups in secure, encrypted locations

## Data Sharing and Transfers

### Third-Party Services

Hisaab does NOT share your data with any third-party services.

### PDF Reports

- When you generate PDF reports, they are created locally on your device
- Sharing reports is entirely under your control
- We recommend being cautious when sharing reports containing sensitive information

## Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

1. **Access**: All your data is accessible within the app
2. **Modification**: You can edit any information at any time
3. **Deletion**: You can delete any customer, transaction, or record
4. **Export**: You can export your data to CSV/Excel format
5. **Portability**: You can create backups and transfer to another device

## Data Retention

- Data is retained on your device until you delete it
- Deleted items are permanently removed (soft delete with option to restore)
- Uninstalling the app will delete all local data
- We recommend regular backups before uninstalling

## Children's Privacy

Hisaab is not intended for use by children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Permissions

The app requires the following permissions:

1. **Storage**: To save and retrieve your business data, backups, and PDF reports
2. **Notifications** (Optional): To send you reminders for pending payments

We only request permissions that are necessary for app functionality.
